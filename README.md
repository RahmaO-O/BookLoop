# BookLoop
>A free platform that connects readers who want to give their books a second life with those looking for their next read.
--

Our app is a free book-exchange platform that helps readers discover and exchange physical books easily. Users can list their books, browse and search for books they want, and use Smart Matching to find potential exchanges. They can send a request to the book owner, chat after acceptance, and choose how to receive the book—either by meeting in person or using a delivery service. The app also includes a Wishlist and notifications to help users find books they are looking for
